ABOUT TECHFEST

TechFest 2026 is a three-day event organized by Pentagon Space at Kukatpally, Hyderabad, from 5th to 7th July 2026.
The fest brings together students and tech enthusiasts through coding competitions, workshops, technical quizzes, project exhibitions, and exciting cultural events.
It provides a platform to learn, showcase talent, build teamwork, and celebrate innovation and creativity.
